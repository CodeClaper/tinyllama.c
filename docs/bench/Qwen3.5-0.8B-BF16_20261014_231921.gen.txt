**Sir Isaac Newton** (1643–1727) was an English mathematician, physicist, astronomer, and philosopher best known as the father of **classical mechanics**. His work fundamentally changed how scientists understand motion, gravity, and light.

### Core Contributions
Newton's most famous theories include:

*   **The Law of Universal Gravitation**: He formulated this principle that every particle of matter in the universe attracts every other particle with a force that is directly proportional to the product of their masses and inversely proportional to the square of the distance between them.
