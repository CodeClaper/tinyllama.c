Issac Newton was an English mathematician, physicist, astronomer and alchemist who lived from 1642 to 1727. He's widely considered one of the greatest scientists in history and his work laid much of the foundations for the modern world.

Newton made important contributions to many scientific fields including optics, classical mechanics, and calculus. His laws of motion and universal gravitation explained how objects move through the universe and how they interact with each other via gravity. Newton's discoveries had a profound impact on science and technology in his lifetime and beyond.
