Sir Isaac Newton (1642-1726) was one of the most influential scientists in history. He made groundbreaking discoveries and inventions that shaped science, mathematics, and engineering.

Some key aspects of his life and work include:

• Born prematurely, he lived only until age 3 but survived to be a brilliant student who excelled at both math and physics
• Developed calculus independently from Gottfried Wilhelm Leibniz (independently discovered)
• Made significant contributions to the fields of mathematics, optics, and mechanics
