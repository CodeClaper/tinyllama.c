Sir Isaac Newton was an English mathematician, physicist, and astronomer who lived from 1642 to 1727. He is widely considered one of the most influential scientists in history, and his work has had a huge impact on many fields, including physics, mathematics, and astronomy.

Newton made fundamental contributions to science, especially in the field of physics. In addition to formulating three laws of motion and the law of universal gravitation that are still used today, he also contributed significantly to the development of calculus, which is a branch of mathematics dealing with rates of change and slopes of curves.
